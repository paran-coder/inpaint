v1.6.0 review and browser adaptations (2026-10-29):
- crates/compose/src/masks.rs: normalized coverage and state-based caching.
- crates/algo/src/selection/morphology.rs: Euclidean radius coverage and canvas-edge behavior.
- crates/algo/src/perspective.rs and transform.rs: quad homography, validation, inverse sampling.
- crates/compose/src/effects.rs: mask-derived shadow shape, shadow-before-content ordering.
- crates/ui-egui/src/dialogs/export_dialog.rs: validated format/quality/size/transparency settings.
- crates/algo/src/tone.rs: straight RGBA processing while retaining alpha.
Differences: browser Canvas renderer, single convex plane constrained inside the layer frame,
8-bit mask coverage, separable EDT, two box passes for selection softening, simple brightness/
warm-cool/luma saturation controls and browser shadow blur/encoding. Not the full Rust engine,
Photoshop effect compatibility, multi-plane warp, or a scene-aware contact-shadow solver.
